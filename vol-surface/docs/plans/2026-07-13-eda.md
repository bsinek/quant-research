## Goal
`notebooks/eda.ipynb` documents how the raw CBOE SPX chain becomes a clean IV
smile/term-structure, and *why* each choice was made — evidence for the decisions
locked this session (root blend, filters, forward). Runs live for anyone; committed
with rendered outputs. IV source = `cboe_iv` (ADR 006, v1 — our own solver is a
later milestone).

## Pipeline (all from `engine/`)
- **Forward:** `forward_by_expiry` (put-call parity, median across liquid strikes).
- **Filters (v1, ADR 008):** `select_otm` + `bidask_mask` + `staleness_mask(7d)`. Volume dropped.
- **Roots:** SPXW until it's exhausted (~1yr), then SPX (LEAPS); prefer SPXW on overlap.
- **Moneyness:** `ln(K/F)`. **Expiries chosen by nearest-to-day-target — never hardcoded** (so it survives a future run).

## Sections (each answers a recruiter question)
1. **Coverage** — clean contracts vs maturity, SPX vs SPXW → *two roots, different coverage.*
2. **Convergence** — median `|IV_SPX − IV_SPXW|` vs **days** at shared monthlies → *justifies the blend; the SPXW→SPX switch is seamless because they've converged (~0.04 vp by ~4mo).*
3. **Forward via parity** — pivot-pair walkthrough + per-strike QC (flat across strikes = parity holds) → *the moneyness axis.*
4. **Filters** — 2×2 breakdown (OTM / bid-ask / staleness / combined) with **volume shown as the rejected one**, then a markdown *"why we keep OTM + bid/ask + staleness, why volume was dropped (asymmetric/harsh intraday)."*
5. **Hero smile** — one expiry (~30d), calls+puts colored, **titled with its filter set.**
6. **Term structure** — 30 / 90 / 180 / 365-day smiles overlaid → *skew flattens with maturity.*
